Payouts

A payout moves your available balance to your bank account. Most payouts arrive in two business days; the first one can take up to seven.

Every payout lists the payments and fees it includes, so you can match it to your bank statement line by line.

GET /v1/payouts?status=in_transit

Payout statuses

StatusMeaning
pendingCreated and waiting for the next payout run.
in_transitSent to the bank; usually arrives in two business days.
paidThe bank confirmed the deposit.
failedThe bank returned it. Check the account details and retry.
