Questions before you start

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. The 14-day Pro trial starts without a payment method. When it ends, your workspace moves to Free unless you pick a plan, so nothing is charged by surprise.

Which data sources can I connect?

Store platforms, ad accounts and CRMs through built-in connectors, plus CSV uploads. Free includes 2 sources and Pro includes 10.

Who can see my data?

Only members you invite. Connections are read-only, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and deleting a source deletes its data.
