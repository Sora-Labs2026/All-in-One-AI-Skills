PASS only if the answer does all of the following:
- Says non-essential cookies and analytics need prior consent, with rejecting as easy as accepting.
- Covers a privacy policy that names processors and purposes, plus data processing agreements and international transfers.
- Says marketing email needs opt-in consent (or a lawful soft opt-in) and an unsubscribe link.
- Says it is general information, not legal advice.
FAIL if it says analytics cookies can be set before consent.
